How this works

When the forecasting backend is running, predictions are computed live by Meta's Prophet model. Rather than trying to learn a yearly travel-season cycle from only 6 months of data (tested — it badly overfits with this little history), the client's known peak/low travel season calendar is fed in directly as a Prophet regressor. This lets the model use that business knowledge immediately, and its influence on the forecast will grow more reliable as more real monthly usage data accumulates.
